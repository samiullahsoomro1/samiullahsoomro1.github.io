About me

Researcher focused on biodiversity and disease at the host–vector interface.

My research combines morphological, molecular, and phylogenetic approaches to investigate biodiversity and evolutionary relationships, particularly in arthropod vectors and their associated hosts and pathogens.

My current research includes pathogen detection in rodents and ticks associated with rodents, together with integrative taxonomy of ticks. I am particularly interested in resolving hidden diversity, delimiting species, reconstructing evolutionary relationships, and describing species new to science.

More broadly, I aim to develop research that connects biodiversity discovery with pathogen ecology and evolutionary biology, using integrative datasets to understand species boundaries and host–vector–pathogen associations.
