Research focus

Seven connected areas of research

My work connects taxonomy and evolutionary biology with molecular pathogen research, with a particular interest in arthropod vectors and their mammalian hosts.

01

Integrative Taxonomy & Systematics

Morphological and molecular evidence for species identification and systematics.

02

Phylogenetics & DNA Barcoding

DNA sequences, phylogenetic relationships, and genetic diversity.

03

Arthropod Vector Taxonomy

Taxonomy and molecular characterization of medically important arthropods.

04

Ticks and Other Ectoparasites

Tick and ectoparasite identification, host associations, and molecular studies.

05

Pathogens in Mammalian Hosts

Molecular detection and characterization of pathogens in animal hosts.

06

Vector-Borne & Zoonotic Pathogens

Host–vector–pathogen relationships and molecular surveillance.

07

Species Discovery & Delimitation

Hidden diversity, species boundaries, and discovery of unrecognized taxa.

Current position

Chulalongkorn University

I am currently a postdoctoral researcher at the Faculty of Veterinary Science, Chulalongkorn University, Bangkok, Thailand, working on tick identification associated with rodents and molecular screening of Bartonella spp. in ticks and rodents.
