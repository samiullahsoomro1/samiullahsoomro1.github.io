About me

Biodiversity researcher connecting taxonomy, evolution, and pathogen research.

During and after my Ph.D., my research has focused on biodiversity, taxonomy and systematics, molecular characterization, DNA barcoding, and phylogenetics, with expertise in arthropods, parasites, and vector-associated pathogens.

I integrate field collections, morphological and molecular approaches, and phylogenetic analyses to investigate species diversity, evolutionary relationships, and systematics of medically and ecologically important organisms.

My research interests include species discovery, species delimitation, integrative taxonomy, and the use of museum collections with modern molecular approaches.

Research

Research interests

01

Integrative taxonomy

Integrating morphological and molecular evidence to investigate species diversity, systematics, and species boundaries in entomology and parasitology.

02

Phylogenetics & DNA barcoding

Phylogenetic analysis, DNA barcoding, sequence comparison, and evolutionary inference to resolve relationships among taxa.

03

Species delimitation

Investigating cryptic and hidden diversity using molecular, morphological, and comparative approaches.

04

Arthropod vectors

Taxonomy and molecular characterization of medically and ecologically important arthropods, including ticks, fleas, and sand flies.

05

Parasites & pathogens

Molecular detection and genetic characterization of vector-associated pathogens and parasites in animal hosts and arthropod vectors.

06

Biodiversity & distribution

Field-based biodiversity research, ecological distribution, genetic diversity, and spatial and temporal patterns.
