About

A researcher working across biodiversity, taxonomy and pathogen research.

My research combines field-based biodiversity studies with morphological taxonomy, molecular characterization, DNA barcoding, phylogenetics, and pathogen detection. I am particularly interested in arthropods of medical and veterinary importance and their associations with mammalian hosts.

My academic trajectory includes zoology, entomology, parasitology, and molecular research, with research experience in Pakistan and Thailand.
