Research interests

These concise research areas summarize the core directions represented across my published research and current work.

01

Integrative Taxonomy & Systematics

Combining morphological, morphometric, molecular, and phylogenetic evidence to resolve species identity, diversity, and systematic relationships.

MorphologyMolecular taxonomySystematics
02

Phylogenetics & DNA Barcoding

DNA barcoding, sequence analysis, phylogenetic reconstruction, genetic diversity, and evolutionary relationships among parasites and arthropods.

DNA barcodingPhylogeneticsGenetic diversity
03

Arthropod Vector Taxonomy

Taxonomy, identification, biodiversity, and molecular characterization of medically and veterinary important arthropods, including sand flies and fleas.

Sand fliesFleasVector taxonomy
04

Ticks and Other Ectoparasites

Tick identification associated with rodents and molecular studies of ectoparasites associated with mammalian hosts.

TicksEctoparasitesHost associations
05

Pathogens in Mammalian Hosts

Molecular detection and genetic characterization of pathogens associated with bats, goats, rodents, and other mammalian hosts.

BatsGoatsRodents
06

Vector-Borne & Zoonotic Pathogens

Investigating host–vector–pathogen relationships through molecular detection, characterization, and phylogenetic analysis.

BartonellaTrypanosomaMycoplasma

Species Discovery & Delimitation

Investigating hidden and unrecognized diversity and evaluating species boundaries using complementary morphological and molecular evidence.

Species discoveryDelimitationCryptic diversity
